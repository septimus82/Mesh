# Icons

Mesh Studio's icons come from **[Lucide](https://lucide.dev)**, version `lucide-static@1.61.0`, under the **ISC
licence** (`lucide/LICENSE`).

- **`lucide/*.svg`:** the original icons, unmodified.
- **`*_16.png`, `*_32.png`:** generated from them by `tooling/make_icons.py` as white glyphs that Studio tints. Run
  that script again after adding an SVG.

**Attribution:** the ISC licence asks that the copyright notice and permission notice stay with copies. They are in
`lucide/LICENSE`, and each SVG keeps its `@license` comment. Nothing else is required: no visible credit, and no
restriction on commercial use.

These icons are third-party and permissively licensed. They are separate from the MeshMind Studios brand assets in
`assets/branding/` (see the `NOTICE.md` there).
