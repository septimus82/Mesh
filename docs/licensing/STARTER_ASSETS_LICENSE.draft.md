## What this licence covers

It covers only the **Mesh starter assets**: files that Mesh's own scripts make, or that were made for Mesh by
MeshMind Studios, and that `assets/NOTICE.md` lists as "Mesh's own". Today these include the HD-2D starter kit
(`assets/hd2d_starter/`, see its `PROVENANCE.md`), the starter characters, sign and tileset, the door, and the
synthesised sounds and music.

**It does not cover any third-party or AI-generated asset.** That includes CC0 recordings, Lucide icons, the MeshMind
Studios brand, and anything made with PixelLab or any other generator. Each keeps its own terms, shown in
`assets/NOTICE.md` and in the notice beside it. This licence does not cover the Mesh Engine's code either.

## What you may do

As a developer using Mesh, you may, free of charge:

1. **Use** the starter assets in your games and other projects, commercial or not.
2. **Modify** them, and combine them with your own work.
3. **Distribute** them, modified or not, **as part of a game or project you make**, in source or compiled form.
4. **Sell** games and projects that contain them.

You don't need to credit Mesh, though a credit is welcome.

## What you may not do

1. Sell or redistribute the starter assets **on their own**, or as the main content of an asset pack, template or
   library, whether modified or not.
2. Use the MeshMind Studios name or brand to suggest that MeshMind Studios endorses your work.

## No warranty

The starter assets are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. MeshMind Studios is not liable for any claim
or damage arising from their use.
