# S1-0 Subsystem Risk Map

**Baseline:** integration tip `7b2b884e`; evidence from parts A–D, s1v and the challenge review. Severity is the highest **open** confirmed defect. Issue numbers are listed under "Confirmed defects"; the class of each is in `10_health_report.md` §3.

**Reading the columns:**
- **Coupling / change-coupling:** fan-in and fan-out, plus co-change counts, from `scan_coupling.py` (1,110 modules) over the last 300–600 non-merge commits.
- **Ownership clarity:** whether one module or path clearly owns the responsibility.
  - **clear:** one owner;
  - **split:** two or more competing implementations;
  - **unclear:** responsibility is spread by mechanism, not by feature.
- **Test reality:** real runtime (a real GameWindow, processes or GL) versus fakes. "0 real" means no test drives the behaviour through a real window (`scan_behaviour_test_coverage.py`; approximate).
- **Reliability:** what was observed when the workflow was driven for real.

## Highest-risk subsystems first

1. **Shipped-game surface:** P0, in flight.
2. **Legacy editor file ops.**
3. **Behaviour construction and authored puzzle/interaction behaviours.**
4. **Project scaffolding and data.**
5. **Persistence of authored state.**

Each of these breaks a creator workflow today. None needs a rewrite: every confirmed defect traces to a small, bounded set of functions. The largest are #651 (three causes across seven files) and the #623 family (five modules).

## Change-coupling hotspots worth watching (no S1 action proposed)

- **`project_ops.py` ↔ `studio_hd2d.py` ↔ `hd2d/renderer.py` ↔ `studio.py`.** Every HD-2D or Studio feature edits 4–6 large files. This is shotgun surgery, but nothing in S1-0 traces a defect to it.
  - **Containment:** none in S1.
  - **Trigger to act:** a defect whose root cause is the coupling itself.
- **`game.py` as god-facade.** Everything reaches through `window.*`, which is the surface where 15 fake-only attributes hid.
  - **Containment:** the meta-test and spec'd fakes (roadmap 5.6), not a split.
- **Scene controller sprawl.** 65 files split by mechanism.
  - **Containment:** none in S1; keep the removal funnel single (D-8).
